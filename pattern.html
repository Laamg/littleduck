The pattern

  1. start 6sc in a magic ring [6]
  2. inc in all 6 [12]
  3. (sc,inc) x6 [18]
  4. sc in all 18 [18]
  5. (sc,inc) x9 [27]
  6. - 10. sc in all 27 [27]

I added the safety eyes between rounds 9 and 10, four stitches apart. The beak is just some yellow yarn sewn over the two stitches in between the eyes.

  1. (sc,dec) x9 [18]
  2. (sc,dec) x6 [12]
  3. dec x6 [6]
  • Stuff with fibrefill.
  • Sew through front loops and tighten to close.
  • Use a small elastic band to gather the first 2-3 rounds into the duck’s tuft (you can use your main yarn to hide the elastic when you weave it in).
